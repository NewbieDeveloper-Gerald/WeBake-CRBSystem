Our Story

Crumbs N' Rolls Bakery

Crumbs N' Rolls Bakery (CRB) is a trusted wholesale bakery established in 2010 by Angelita B. Amadeo. Located at 1356 Cordero Street, Lambakin, Marilao, Bulacan, our bakery was founded with a heartfelt purpose: to provide quality, freshly baked, and affordable bread products while supplying fellow partner bakeries and creating a sustainable livelihood for our family and community.

Over the years, CRB Bakery has grown into a cornerstone wholesale supplier for neighborhood bakeries, cafes, and retail stores, while continuing to warmly serve local walk-in customers whenever fresh batches emerge from our ovens.

Our operations are powered by a dedicated team of eight skilled bakers and personnel handling daily baking, hygienic packaging, and prompt early-morning deliveries across key regions.
