About Us

Crumbs N' Rolls Bakery is a wholesale bakery owned by Angelita B. Amadeo and was established in 2010. It is located at 1356 Cordero Street, Lambakin, Marilao, Bulacan.

The business was founded to provide quality and affordable bread products while supplying partner bakeries and creating a stable source of income for the owner's family.

CRB Bakery primarily operates as a wholesale bakery that supplies bread products to other bakeries, while also accommodating walk-in customers whenever products are available. The bakery currently employs eight staff members who are involved in production, packaging, and delivery operations.

Its products are distributed to various areas, including Caloocan, Ilocos, and Malolos, allowing the bakery to serve a wide range of customers and business partners.
